One line in the voice of the Moon, replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it — entered in the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) bounty "The Moon Finally Answers Back". A house judge reads the lines two at a time and picks the funniest; the five best lines share the $2 pool.

> "Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 3 a.m. promises — and not one of you has ever asked how I am doing."

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), with no trailing newline. Python 3 standard library only, deterministic — running it twice produces byte-identical output.
